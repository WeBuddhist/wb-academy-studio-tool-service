## The task

Below is what the teacher says in this lesson. Write, in Tibetan, for a learner who has just listened to it:

- `summary`: 2 to 4 sentences on what this lesson teaches.
- `key_points`: 3 to 5 key points, each one short sentence.
- `mcq`: 2 or 3 multiple-choice questions that check the learner understood this lesson. Each has 3 or 4
  `options`, all different, with exactly one right answer; `answer` is the position of the right option,
  counting from 0. The other options are plausible but clearly wrong for someone who listened. `explanation`
  says in one sentence why the answer is right, from the teaching. Ask only about what this lesson says.
- `reflection`: 3 open questions for the learner to reflect on, about how this teaching applies to their own
  mind and life. These have no right answer.
